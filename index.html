InterCom-Office Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026
Developer: VA-GR  |  Contact: kachakra1997@gmail.com,

InterCom-Office is an office chat, file-sharing and network tool. It works directly between computers on your own network. We run no server and receive no data from you. We do not collect analytics, advertising IDs or crash reports.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your display name and settings.
  • People, sections, device lists (names, IP and MAC addresses of devices found on your network), and your saved network profiles.
  • Chat history, stored encrypted with Windows so that only your Windows user can read it.
  • Files you received, in the Documents\Received From folder (delete them yourself) or kept inside the app if you chose that.

This data is kept in your Windows user profile (the folder AppData\Roaming\InterCom) and in Documents\Received From. Uninstalling the app may leave these folders behind; delete them yourself if you want everything gone.

What is sent over the network, and to whom

  • To other InterCom-Office users you choose or that are on your network: your name and online status, your messages and the files you send. Chats and all file transfers are end-to-end encrypted; the app refuses unencrypted file transfers. Screen sharing is encrypted too.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted: the short pop-up "Message", and the small announcements (name, status) that computers on the same network send so they can find each other.
  • To scan for devices, the app sends probes (ping, port checks, name lookups) to addresses on your own local networks.
  • Optional: the internet speed test contacts Cloudflare, and the connection check contacts Microsoft and a few public test sites. Those services see your IP address under their own policies.

Camera, microphone and screen

Used only for calls, meetings and screen sharing that you start or accept. Screen sharing and remote control always need the other person's explicit permission every time (where the edition includes them); Windows shows a border around the shared screen and the app shows a red bar with a Stop button. Nothing is recorded or uploaded by us.

Changing network settings

Network profiles can change this computer's IP address and DNS. This needs administrator permission, so Windows asks you and the app shows exactly what will change; it happens only when you press "Switch to this network". The app does not change settings on its own, and does not change the hosts file, computer name or firewall.

Start with Windows

Off by default. If you turn it on, Windows records your permission and you can withdraw it in Windows Settings at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Turn sending and receiving off, block people, mute chats, or uninstall at any time.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
